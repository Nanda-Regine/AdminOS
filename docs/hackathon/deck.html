AdminOS

The WhatsApp-native business OS that takes a young South African founder from “I have an idea” to “I run a business”.

Mirembe Muse (Pty) Ltd · East London, Eastern Cape · Nandawula Regine, founder
Live at adminos.co.za · github.com/Nanda-Regine/AdminOS

The problem

Starting a business is the easy part. Staying alive is what nobody teaches.

💸 Cash is trapped

Customers owe money, the founder is too polite (or too busy) to chase it, and rent is due on the 1st.

⚠️ Deadlines nobody warned them about

SARS, CIPC and COIDA penalties arrive before the founder knows the deadline existed.

📓 No records, no funding

A WhatsApp chat and a notebook can't show a lender, a funder or a bank that the business is real.

📱 Five apps they can't afford

Invoicing here, payroll there, chat somewhere else — built for US businesses, not for load-shedding and prepaid data.

AdminOS03
The product · live today

Every morning, one screen instead of five apps.

  • What needs a decision right now
  • Cash owed, overdue and stuck
  • Goals, compliance and team at a glance
  • Built mobile-first, works on a 390px Android
1 · Get paid

The cash you’ve already earned — collected.

Cash Cockpit tells a founder plainly what they’re owed, what’s stuck, and who to chase first.

  • Branded tax invoices & payment receipts
  • Quick Sale for cash and walk-in customers
  • 5-tier automated debt recovery agent
  • Every rand categorised to a chart of accounts
1 · Get paid — the bank loop

Your bank statement, closed into the books.

  • Upload a CSV from FNB, Capitec, ABSA, Standard Bank, Nedbank or TymeBank
  • Preview first; lines already imported are skipped
  • Spending sorts itself into categories and learns your vendors
  • Payments are matched to the invoices they paid, by invoice number, payer name and the exact amount owed
  • One tap settles the invoice. Look-alikes and part-payments are flagged, never guessed
2 · Stay compliant

The fear every founder has — answered in one click.

  • 19 SARS, CIPC and COIDA obligations, pre-loaded
  • Dates built on SARS-verified rules, rolled forward monthly
  • The actual penalty for missing each one
  • POPIA consent built into every broadcast

No other small-business tool knows what an EMP201 is.

3 · Know your numbers

Books that are in order from day one.

  • Business health score and daily AI brief
  • Cashflow, expenses and payables in one place
  • Accountant-ready exports: income statement, VAT, journal CSV
  • Payroll with PAYE, UIF and SDL
4 · Grow

Run the whole business — not just the books.

Bookings & customers — a public booking link and a 24h reminder.

Stock & suppliers — reorder alerts and a B-BBEE supplier scorecard.

Team & tasks — staff, leave, tasks and goals with progress.
